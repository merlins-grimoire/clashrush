# Standing non-premium currency policy

## 5C — Indefinite, unlimited non-premium currency approval

The owner grants indefinite approval, until explicitly revoked, for spending **ALL
non-premium in-game currencies**. Included are Home Gold, Home Elixir, Dark Elixir,
Builder Gold, Builder Elixir, Capital Gold, medals (including Raid/League-style
medals), event currencies/tokens, and ordinary matchmaking/search fees paid in
approved non-premium currency. No numeric or cumulative cap applies to these
currency classes.

Still prohibited: Gems, real-money purchases, external payment, credentials/account-
security changes, and use/consumption of non-currency inventory items merely because
their acquisition used an approved currency. No gem fallback is permitted when
non-premium funds are insufficient.

Currency approval does **not** authorize a new gameplay feature, transaction type,
account switch, live attempt, executor or runtime admission. A spend may occur only
when the underlying capability/action is separately authorized, the exact account/
instance/run is authorized, the executor is reviewed for that transaction category,
and the observed target/cost/result satisfy that capability's contract. Unknown or
ambiguous currency identity, target, cost or outcome cannot be guessed. An unknown
currency must first be established as non-premium; this is not a five-resource-only
allowlist or a blanket ban on event currencies.

This policy supersedes older independent currency-enablement/cap recommendations,
including the reconciliation package's different meaning for option 5C. It does not
add executors or change production configuration/code. Transaction-specific feature
gates remain separate: approval of medals does not activate reinforcement, and
approval of event currency does not activate crafting. Home hero upgrades and
laboratory research remain intended Dark Elixir product scope; pets remain deferred.

Finite future attempts/time bounds govern live authority, not currency amounts.
See [standing workflow](../governance/development-workflow.md), the
[inactive live template](../governance/live-development-envelope-template.md), and
[current evidence](../architecture/CURRENT.md). No live permission is granted here.
